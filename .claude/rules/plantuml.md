# Guidelines for actualizing UML diagrams

## Digram `doc/uml/ai/packages.puml`

1. Create a PlantUML diagram of dependencies between folders in `src`.
2. Do not show self-dependencies.
3. Do not show dependencies on its own inner folders.
4. Do not show dependencies on own parent folders.
5. Make narrows red.
